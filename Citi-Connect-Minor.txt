INTRODUCTION
With the growth of digital governance, citizens expect efficient and transparent platforms to submit and track public grievances. However, existing grievance redressal systems are often manual, slow, and lack real-time communication, anonymity, and accountability. This project presents an Smart Grievance Redressal System that enables secure complaint submission, real-time tracking, and anonymous interaction between citizens and authorities. By integrating AI-powered chatbots, NLP-based analysis, location services, emergency SOS, and identity protection features, the system enhances transparency, responsiveness, and citizen trust in governance.

EXISTING SYSTEM & LIMITATIONS
Existing System:
The existing grievance redressal systems are primarily based on traditional web portals, manual complaint registers, or email-based communication channels. In many government and institutional setups, citizens are required to submit grievances through static online forms, physical complaint boxes, or by visiting offices in person. These systems usually provide limited tracking mechanisms, where users receive minimal updates on complaint status, often relying on manual follow-ups or delayed responses from authorities. Most current platforms lack real-time communication between citizens and officials, and grievance processing is largely manual, leading to increased resolution time. Identity disclosure is often mandatory, which discourages citizens from reporting sensitive issues due to fear of retaliation or privacy concerns. Additionally, existing systems rarely support multimedia evidence such as images or videos, and if supported, they lack automated privacy-preserving mechanisms. Analytical capabilities are also limited, with authorities having minimal tools to assess complaint trends, performance metrics, or departmental workloads. Emergency reporting and intelligent assistance through chatbots or automated guidance are generally absent in conventional grievance systems.

Limitations:
Limited transparency and delayed grievance updates
Manual processing increases resolution time
No support for anonymous complaint submission
Lack of AI-based categorization and analytics
Poor scalability for handling large complaint volumes

PROPOSED SYSTEM
The proposed system is a smart, secure, and scalable grievance management platform aimed at enhancing transparency, efficiency, and accountability in public grievance redressal. The system is designed with separate interfaces for citizens and authorities to ensure structured interaction and effective complaint resolution.

Citizens can submit grievances through a unified portal by providing detailed descriptions along with supporting images, videos, or documents.
An AI-based face blurring mechanism automatically anonymizes faces in uploaded images and videos to protect user identity.
An NLP-based module analyzes and categorizes complaints and notices to improve clarity, organization, and processing efficiency.
The system provides an anonymous chat feature that enables secure, real-time communication between citizens and authorities without revealing identities.
Authorities are provided with a centralized dashboard to view, prioritize, and resolve pending grievances efficiently.
An Emergency SOS feature allows users to send instant alerts for urgent situations to the concerned authorities.
An analytics dashboard helps authorities visualize complaint trends, resolution time, and departmental performance.
All grievance records and multimedia evidence are securely stored using cloud storage for scalability and reliability

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Traditional grievance redressal mechanisms in government and institutional systems are largely manual, fragmented, and time-consuming. Citizens are often required to submit complaints through physical visits, static web portals, or email-based systems that provide limited transparency and delayed updates. The lack of real-time tracking, secure communication, and structured complaint handling leads to inefficiencies, poor accountability, and reduced trust in the grievance resolution process.Additionally, existing systems typically require mandatory identity disclosure, discouraging individuals from reporting sensitive or critical issues due to privacy and safety concerns. The absence of intelligent tools such as NLP-based complaint categorization, analytics, and automated assistance further increases the workload on authorities and delays resolution. These limitations highlight the need for a secure, scalable, and citizen-centric grievance management platform that supports anonymity, real-time interaction, intelligent analysis, and transparent monitoring to improve public service delivery and accountability.
